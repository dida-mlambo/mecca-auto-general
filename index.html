INSIDE OUR WORKSHOP
01 — ABOUT US

Your trusted partner in automotive excellence

Mecca Auto & General is a premier automotive service provider specialising in genuine parts and exceptional repair services for Toyota, Isuzu, Ford and Nissan vehicles. Founded on integrity and excellence, we've built a reputation as a leader in Zimbabwe's automotive industry — with a steadfast commitment to quality, safety and environmental sustainability.

Every vehicle, and every owner, has different requirements. Our technicians give each customer personalised attention and tailored solutions that enhance performance and reliability — not a one-size-fits-all service.

Outstanding customer service sits at the core of everything we do. With an expanding range of services, a dedicated team, and a vast inventory of reliable spare parts, we're positioned as a leading partner for individual vehicle owners and organisations alike.
